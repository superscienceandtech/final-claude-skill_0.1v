# Member 3 Scope Clarification

## Decision

P098 used Outcome B: no authoritative role-wide Member 3 assignment was
recovered from the repository. Further implementation work that depends on
role-wide scope must remain blocked until the project owner confirms the
assignment or supplies its authoritative source.

## What is established

- The P094–P098 task instructions supplied in the conversation define
  milestone-specific objectives, acceptance criteria, and exclusions. They do
  not, by themselves, establish the complete role-wide Member 3 assignment.
- [`workflows/spec-to-implementation/README.md`](../workflows/spec-to-implementation/README.md)
  describes a provider-neutral implementation and verification workflow
  intended for Member 3 work. It is supporting process guidance, not a complete
  role assignment.
- [`skills/implementation/implementation.md`](../skills/implementation/implementation.md),
  [`README.md`](../README.md), and [`skills/README.md`](../skills/README.md)
  describe repository and implementation boundaries, not a complete Member 3
  task allocation.
- [`docs/member-3-acceptance-matrix.md`](./member-3-acceptance-matrix.md)
  records milestone-specific implementation and verification evidence, with
  prior P096 test results explicitly identified as historical session
  evidence.
- [`docs/skills/provisional-structural-validator-spec.md`](./skills/provisional-structural-validator-spec.md)
  is explicitly provisional and not implementation authorization.
- The repository search found no separate Member 3 assignment, charter, task
  allocation, approved role-wide acceptance specification, or pre-existing
  canonical acceptance matrix.

## What remains unknown

- The full responsibilities and exclusions assigned to Member 3.
- Which workstreams, interfaces, or deliverables Member 3 owns versus depends
  on other members or project owners.
- The authoritative acceptance criteria and required evidence for role-wide
  delivery.
- Any priority, dependency, approval, or milestone sequencing rules beyond
  those expressly given in the P094–P098 task instructions.

## Minimum confirmation needed

Before authorizing further role-wide implementation, the project owner should:

1. Provide the authoritative assignment/charter/task-allocation source, or
   confirm that the supplied milestone instructions are the complete scope.
2. Confirm Member 3 ownership, boundaries, and exclusions, including any
   dependencies or required approvals from other members.
3. Confirm the acceptance criteria and verification evidence required for the
   remaining deliverables.
4. Identify any required milestone order or blocking dependencies.

No implementation gap is inferred from the missing assignment. The existing
P094–P098 milestone boundaries and exclusions remain as stated in their task
instructions; this document does not expand or override them.

## Copyable request to the project owner

> Please provide or identify the authoritative Member 3 assignment, charter, or
> task-allocation source, or confirm that the P094–P098 milestone instructions
> are the complete Member 3 scope. Please also confirm Member 3 ownership and
> exclusions, any cross-member dependencies or required approvals, and the
> acceptance criteria and verification evidence for any remaining work. If
> milestones must be completed in a particular order, please identify the
> blocking dependencies. No further implementation will be started until this
> scope is confirmed.
