### Independent evaluation prerequisite

Any future independent evaluation requires a genuinely separate evaluator,
identified inputs and expected outcomes, and a recorded run. No evaluator is
identified by the operating-model decision.

### Implementation evidence prerequisite

An instructed implementer may assess technical feasibility only under
explicit scope authorization. A response or self-review is not independent
evaluation or Owner acceptance.

## Authorization boundary

This decision aid is documentation only. It creates no Skill, validator,
fixture, or approval. The existing bounded Owner authorizations remain limited
to their recorded scope; repository-audit execution and evaluation remain
gated unless their specific required authorization is established.
