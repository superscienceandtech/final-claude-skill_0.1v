## Hourly, shift and plant-floor employment

The obligations above apply everywhere. An hourly, shift-based workforce concentrates the exposure
in a few mechanisms, and they are mechanical rather than judgmental — which means errors are
systematic, apply to everyone at once, and accumulate quietly until they are found in aggregate.

**Time worked is not time on the clock, and not time scheduled.** Compensable time typically starts
before the workstation: donning required protective equipment, walking to a post where that
equipment is required, pre-shift handover, and mandatory briefings. Rounding rules that are neutral
in design are frequently not neutral in practice, and the test is whether they favor the employer
over a period.

**Overtime is calculated on a regular rate that is wider than the base rate.** Shift differentials,
non-discretionary production or attendance bonuses and similar payments generally have to be folded
in before the premium is computed. Paying the premium on base pay alone is the most common
underpayment in a plant, and because it is arithmetic it applies to every affected employee for the
whole look-back period.

**Breaks, meal periods and rest are jurisdictional and specific.** Duration, timing, whether they are
paid, and whether an interrupted meal period becomes compensable vary by state and country. A single
national policy will be wrong somewhere, and usually in the direction that costs.

**Temporary and agency workers can create joint employment.** Where the site controls the work,
schedules the hours and supervises directly, obligations can attach to the site regardless of who
issues the paycheck. Assume exposure and confirm the agency's compliance rather than the reverse.

Classification of production supervisors is the recurring judgment call: a working supervisor who
spends most of a shift on the line may not meet the exemption tests whatever the title says.

Attendance and scheduling systems encode all of this. When the policy is right and the configuration
is old, the configuration wins — and it is the configuration that will be produced in discovery.
